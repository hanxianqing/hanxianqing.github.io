About Merry Printables

Merry Printables is a small online shop selling printable party games, Christmas games and planners as instant PDF downloads. Buy a bundle on Payhip, download it right away, print it at home, and play tonight — nothing is shipped, and the free samples and tools need no sign-up at all.

What we make

Everything in the shop is a print-at-home PDF: Christmas party games, a Christmas printables mega pack with bingo, trivia and gift tags, New Year's Eve games, and a 2027 goal & habit planner. Every product page lists exactly what is inside — page by page, game by game — so you can count the value before you buy. And every bundle has a free sample you can print first: see free printables.

We also make free tools that run entirely in your browser: a Christmas bingo card generator, a Christmas word search maker and a Christmas countdown. Nothing is uploaded, nothing needs an account.

Our promises

1. Ready to print

Every PDF is laid out for US Letter paper (8.5 × 11 in) and prints fine on A4. Print at 100% — "Actual size" in your print settings — and the cards and tags come out the right size. Card stock makes bingo cards and gift tags last the whole party.

2. What you see is what's inside

Each product page lists every page, every game and every answer key before you buy — no surprises after checkout. If you want to check the quality first, print the free sample: it is a real page from the paid bundle.

3. Printing guidance included

Every product page has a how-to-play (or how-to-use) section and printing tips: paper size, print scale, what to cut out, and which pages work best on card stock.

4. Help if something goes wrong

If a file is damaged or will not open or print, message us through Payhip (use the contact link on the product page) and tell us which file it is — we will send you a replacement. Please message us before leaving a review so we can fix it first.

How to reach us
