How to use the planner

  1. Print the whole planner, or just the months you need — every month is a separate page.
  2. Start on the goals page: pick your Word of the Year and your top 5 goals for 2027.
  3. Choose one habit to track each month and tick it off daily.
  4. Review at month end: what worked, what to change, then print next month.
