Questions

Which mic should a beginner buy?

The FIFINE K669B if the budget is tight, or the Samson Q2U if you can spend more — the Q2U works over USB now and over XLR later, so it grows with you.

Why is the RØDE listed as “around $100”?

Because sources disagree: $99.99 in one place, $105 in others. It hovers at or just over $100, so we do not claim it is safely under $100 — check the price before you buy.

Do I need anything else besides the mic?

A quiet room matters more than accessories. Headphones help you hear yourself, and a pop filter helps with plosives — but the mic alone is enough to start.
