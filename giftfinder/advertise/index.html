Questions

How much does a placement cost?

Placement is free during our launch period. Once the site has traffic, sponsored placements and ads will be paid. We don't publish rates yet because we don't have traffic figures to base them on.

Will the Featured Gift look like a recommendation?

No. The Featured Gift card always carries a “Featured Gift — Sponsored” badge, a gold border and a gold-tinted background. It never replaces an organic pick and is never presented as our recommendation.

What won't you do?

No disguised recommendations, no fake reviews, and no invented prices. A sponsored product is described in the sponsor's own words, checked by us, and priced only if the price is verified.

How do I enquire?

Use the button below — it goes to the Merry Printables store, where the store's contact route lives. Tell us which placement you're interested in and what you'd like to feature — we'll reply from the store.
