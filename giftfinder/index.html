Questions

How does the Gift Finder work?

Pick a recipient, an interest and a budget. The finder shows 3 specific gifts from our catalog — our own printable packs first where one fits, then Amazon products. Every gift has a reason and a real link.

Do prices change?

Yes. Every price on this site was checked on 20 Nov 2026 and shows its source next to it. Three of the prices are Black Friday deal prices and may rise after 27 Nov 2026. Always check the price before you buy.

Why do some gifts say “Price on Amazon” with no number?

Because we could not verify a trustworthy current price for them. We would rather show no number than a stale or guessed one. Click through to Amazon to see the current price.

Do you earn money from the Amazon links?

Not yet. Amazon Associates is not registered, so all Amazon links are plain links with no affiliate tags. We earn nothing from them. Our own printable packs are sold through Payhip, and those Buy buttons are the only ones that earn us money.

Do I need an account or an email address?

No. The finder, the guides and the budget calculator all work with no sign-up and no email. Buying a printable pack checks out on Payhip, which handles payment itself.
