Questions

What should I not buy a teacher?

Another mug, unless you know they collect them. Classroom supplies and printable games get used; novelty gifts usually do not.

Are the classroom packs printable at school?

Yes — they are PDF downloads for US Letter paper. Print at home or at school; they are made for personal and classroom use.
