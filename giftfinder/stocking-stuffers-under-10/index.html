Not all of them, honestly. The two Payhip packs are $6, and the free bingo sample is free. The NeeDoh cube and the Yankee Candle car jar have no verified price, so we show none. The UNO game is $10.49 only on a Black Friday deal and usually costs $14–$16.

Why do some gifts say “Price on Amazon” with no number?

Because we could not verify a trustworthy current price. The NeeDoh price moved between $9.99 and $14.99 in a few days, and the car jar price we found was from a crawl 258 days old. We would rather show no number than a stale one.
