Questions

Is this financial advice?

No. This is a planning estimate, not financial advice. It only adds up the numbers you type in — it does not know your income, debts or other commitments. Prices change — check before you buy.

What does the total budget cap do?

If you set a total cap lower than per-person budget times people, the cap wins: the calculator lowers your per-person figure to fit the cap, and says so in the result.

What if I have already bought some gifts?

Type what you have spent so far into “already bought”. The calculator subtracts it and shows what is left — the bar shows spent versus remaining.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. The calculator runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type leaves your device, and there is no sign-up or email gate on the result.

What fits my per-person budget?

The result lists our printable packs at or under your per-person figure, and links to the Gift Finder with your budget pre-selected.
