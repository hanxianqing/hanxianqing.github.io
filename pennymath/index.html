Questions, answered

Are the calculators really free?

Yes. All four calculators are free, with no sign-up and no email. The printable pack is a separate paid product for people who want the same planning on paper.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No. Every calculation runs in your browser with JavaScript. There is no server, no account and no analytics on this site.

How accurate are the results?

They are estimates. The mortgage maths is exact for the numbers you type in, but tax figures use simplified published brackets and your real paycheque depends on deductions this calculator does not know about. Check the current figures with a professional before you act on them.

Why do you only cover five states on the salary calculator?

Each state has its own tax rules, and covering all fifty properly would take far longer than the calculator is worth. California and New York are the two states with the most complex income tax; Texas, Florida and Washington have no state income tax at all. More states may be added later.
