How to play

  1. Print one game sheet per player — or one per team for big groups.
  2. Start with Would You Rather: it needs no explaining and gets everyone talking.
  3. Play 2–3 games per party. Family Feud and Scattergories work best in teams; Emoji Pictionary works for all ages.
  4. Keep score on the score sheet and hand out a small prize after each game.

Party tips

  • Split big groups into teams of 4–6 — nobody waits long for a turn.
  • Read the Left–Right story slowly so the gifts actually move.
  • Charades cards are funnier on card stock — print, cut, fold, and draw from a bowl.
  • End on Finish the Carol: everyone sings, nobody loses.
