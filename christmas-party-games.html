How to play

  1. Buy and download. Right after checkout, Payhip shows your download link on the confirmation page and emails it to you too — one 12-page PDF, instantly.
  2. Print one game sheet per player — or one per team for big groups. Print at 100% (Actual size) on US Letter paper; use card stock for the charades cards so they survive being drawn from a bowl.
  3. Start with Would You Rather. It needs no explaining and gets everyone talking — read each pair aloud, everyone picks A or B and says why, and the funniest reason wins the round.
  4. Play 2–3 games per party. Family Feud and Scattergories work best in teams; Emoji Pictionary works for all ages. Keep score on the score sheet and hand out a small prize after each game.
  5. End on Finish the Carol. Everyone sings, nobody loses — and the answer key stays with the host until the end.

Party tips

  • Split big groups into teams of 4–6 — nobody waits long for a turn.
  • Read the Left–Right story slowly so the gifts actually move.
  • Charades cards are funnier on card stock — print, cut, fold, and draw from a bowl.
  • End on Finish the Carol: everyone sings, nobody loses.
