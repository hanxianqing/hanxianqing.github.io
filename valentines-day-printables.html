How to play

  1. Buy and download. Right after checkout, Payhip shows your download link on the confirmation page and emails it to you too — one 18-page PDF, instantly.
  2. Print at 100% (Actual size) on US Letter paper — never “Fit to page”. Use card stock for the Valentine cards and bingo cards, plain paper for the rest.
  3. Cut out the Valentine cards and the 30 bingo calling cards. Fold the calling cards and drop them in a bowl; keep the trivia answer key for the host.
  4. Start with Would You Rather to get everyone talking, then bingo, then trivia in teams.
  5. Finish with the scavenger hunt for the kids and the colouring page for the quiet ones — and give every guest a Valentine card to take home.

Party tips

  • Pick 2–3 games, not all of them — a short, lively session beats a long, tired one.
  • Have small prizes ready: chocolates, stickers, or a silly trophy for the bingo winner.
  • Print the night before, not the morning of — printers always jam on the day.
  • One answer key stays with the host; players get game sheets only.
