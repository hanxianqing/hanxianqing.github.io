Printing tips

  • Print at 100% / "Actual size" so nothing gets trimmed.
  • Use card stock for bingo cards, charades cards and gift tags — they survive the party.
  • Print one answer key for the host, plus one game sheet per guest.
  • Test-print one page first if your printer is new to you.
