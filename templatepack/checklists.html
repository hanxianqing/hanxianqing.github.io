Good to know

  • Assign one owner per item — a checklist with no owner does not get done.
  • Review the list after each new hire or new client, and fix the items that never get ticked.
  • Keep the IT-setup items separate — they are their own checklist in the Pro Pack.
  • A checklist is a starting point, not a policy — adapt it to your team.

Questions

Can I print these checklists?

Yes. Press “Print this checklist” on the checklist you need — it prints with empty boxes to tick on paper.

Do my ticks save?

Ticks work on the page and in print, but they are not saved between visits — there is no account, so nothing is stored. Print the list if you want to keep your ticks.

Can I share this with my team?

Yes. Copy the list or print it and hand it out. The Pro Pack has the same checklists as Word files, so you can save your team's standard version.

What other checklists are in the Pro Pack?

The Pro Pack has 10 checklists: the two on this page plus new-hire IT setup, project launch, event planning, monthly bookkeeping close, website launch, product launch, business-trip travel and end-of-year business review.

Can I edit the checklists?

Yes. Copy the list, paste it into your own document, and add or remove items to fit your team.
