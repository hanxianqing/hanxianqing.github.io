Free Business Plan Templates

Six free business plan templates, from the SBA's guided planner to a one-page lean plan. A plan is a decision tool, not a document to file away.

A business plan answers five questions: what you sell, who buys it, how you reach them, what it costs, and what you make. Investors read the financials; you read the rest every month to check whether the plan still matches reality. The templates below cover both the full plan and the one-page version.

Questions

How long should a business plan be?

A lean plan fits on one page. A full plan for investors runs 15-25 pages. Write the lean plan first - if you cannot explain the business on one page, twenty pages will not fix it.

Do I need a business plan to start?

You need the answers, not the document. If you are not raising money, a one-page plan plus a simple budget is enough to start.

What do investors actually read?

The executive summary, the market, and the financials - in that order. Most of the rest is there to show you have thought it through.
