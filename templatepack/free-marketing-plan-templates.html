Questions

What goes in a marketing plan?

The audience, the channels, the message, the budget and how you will measure it. A plan without a budget number and a measurement is a wish list.

How much should a small business spend on marketing?

A common rule is 5-10% of revenue for established businesses, more while growing. The honest answer: what you can measure. If you cannot tell whether it worked, spend less until you can.

One-page plan or full plan?

One page for a small business or a single campaign. A full plan only when several people need to coordinate and the budget is large enough to argue about.
