Questions

Should I use a different resume for every job?

Yes, at least a lightly tailored one. Keep one master resume with everything, then cut and reorder it for each role so the most relevant experience is on the first half of page one.

What format should I download my resume in?

PDF for sending to people, because it looks the same everywhere. Word (.docx) for uploading to systems that parse resumes - some applicant tracking systems read Word better than PDF.

Is a one-page resume better?

For most roles under ten years of experience, yes. Hiring managers skim; a second page is fine if the experience is genuinely relevant, but a third page is almost never read.
