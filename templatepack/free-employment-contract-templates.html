Free Employment Contract Templates

Six free employment contract templates. A contract template is a starting point - get local legal advice before you use one.

An employment contract sets pay, hours, notice, and what happens when things end. Templates save time, but employment law is local: a template written for one country can be unenforceable in another. Use these to draft, then have a local lawyer or HR adviser review before signing.

TemplatePack does not publish an employment contract template - contracts are legal documents and need local review. The sources above are the best free starting points we found. Templates on this site are wording suggestions, not legal advice.

Questions

Can I use a free employment contract template as-is?

As a draft, yes. As a signed contract, only after someone who knows your local employment law has reviewed it. Employment law varies by country and state.

What must an employment contract include?

Job title, duties, pay, hours, place of work, notice period, and any probation terms. Anything else depends on the role and the jurisdiction.

Is a verbal agreement enough?

In some places, yes - but it is very hard to prove. A written contract protects both sides, and it is cheap insurance against a dispute.
