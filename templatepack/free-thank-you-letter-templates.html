Questions

When should I send a thank-you letter after an interview?

The same day, or the next morning at the latest. After 48 hours it reads as an afterthought.

Email or handwritten?

Email for speed - it arrives while the decision is being made. A handwritten note is a nice extra, not a replacement.

What should the letter actually say?

Thank them for their time, name one thing from the conversation that excited you, and restate briefly why you fit. Three short paragraphs.
