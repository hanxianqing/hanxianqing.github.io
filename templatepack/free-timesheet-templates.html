Questions

Weekly or daily timesheets?

Daily logging, weekly totals. Hours logged at the end of the week are guesses; hours logged the same day are records.

What should a timesheet include?

Date, client or project, task, start and end time, and total hours. A notes column helps when a client asks what the hours were for.

Do I need a timesheet if I charge a fixed fee?

Yes, for yourself. It tells you whether the fixed fee is profitable - and it is the evidence if a client asks for more work inside the same fee.
