Questions

How do I copy a Google Sheets template?

Open the template link, then File, then Make a copy. The copy lands in your Drive and the original stays untouched.

Can I use Google Sheets templates offline?

Yes, with the Google Sheets offline setting turned on in Chrome. Otherwise Sheets needs an internet connection.

Will Excel templates work in Google Sheets?

Mostly. Upload an .xlsx file to Drive and open it with Sheets; simple formulas convert fine, but complex Excel formulas and macros may not.
