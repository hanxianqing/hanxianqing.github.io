Questions

Expense tracker or budget - what is the difference?

A budget is the plan (what you intend to spend); an expense tracker is the record (what you actually spent). You need both, and the tracker is what makes the budget honest.

How detailed should categories be?

Ten to fifteen categories is the sweet spot. Fewer and the totals tell you nothing; more and you stop logging because every entry is a decision.

Should I track cash spending too?

Yes, if it is more than a coffee a week. Untracked cash is the usual reason a budget looks fine on paper and the bank balance disagrees.
