Questions

Are the templates free to use at work?

Yes. Every template on this site is free to copy and use at work, as often as you like. There is no sign-up and no email gate.

Do I need to credit TemplatePack?

No. Copy the text, replace the [brackets] with your own details, and send it as your own. The Pro Pack is the same idea as files.

Can I edit the templates?

Yes — that is the point. Every template has [bracketed] placeholders for the parts that change: names, dates, amounts, companies.
