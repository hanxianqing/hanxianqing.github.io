Questions

How long should a cover letter be?

Three or four short paragraphs on one page. Hiring managers read the first paragraph and skim the rest - put your strongest reason in the opening lines.

Do I still need a cover letter in 2026?

When the application asks for one, yes. When it is optional, a short one still helps for roles where motivation matters - career changes, small companies, and roles with many similar applicants.

Should the cover letter match my resume design?

Yes. Same font, same heading style, same name block. It signals care, and it makes the two documents read as one application.
