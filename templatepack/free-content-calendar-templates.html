Questions

How far ahead should a content calendar go?

One month of firm dates and one quarter of ideas. Further out than that, the calendar is guessing, not planning.

What columns does a content calendar need?

Date, title or topic, channel, owner, and status. Add a link column once the piece is published, so the calendar doubles as your archive.

Spreadsheet or a dedicated tool?

A spreadsheet until you publish more than ten pieces a month or work with more than three people. Then a tool with approvals earns its keep.
