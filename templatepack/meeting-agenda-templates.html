How to use

  1. Copy the agenda. click Copy on the agenda you need.
  2. Replace the [brackets]. with your names, dates and topics.
  3. Send it before the meeting. the day before is best.

Team Weekly Meeting Agenda

Use this when: the regular Monday team meeting

Welcome and quick wins (5 min)
Each person shares one win from last week.

Progress updates (15 min)
Each person: what they finished, what they are working on, what is blocked.

Decisions needed (10 min)
[Decision 1] — who decides, by when. [Decision 2].

Risks and blockers (10 min)
What is stuck, who can unblock it, and by when.

Next week (5 min)
Top three priorities for next week. Owner for each.

Any other business (5 min)
Anything not covered above.

One-to-One Meeting Agenda

Use this when: your regular 1:1 with a team member

Check-in (5 min)
How are things? Workload, energy, anything on your mind.

Progress since last time (10 min)
What moved forward, what stalled, and why.

Feedback (10 min)
One thing that went well, one thing to improve — both directions.

Goals and development (10 min)
Progress on goals, training, or the next step in your role.

Support needed (5 min)
What do you need from me this week?

Actions
Write down who does what by when before you finish.

Project Kickoff Meeting Agenda

Use this when: the first meeting of a new project

Welcome and introductions (10 min)
Who is in the room, and what is each person's role on this project.

The goal (10 min)
What success looks like, in one sentence. How we will know we got there.

Scope — what is in, what is out (15 min)
List the deliverables. List what we are explicitly not doing.

Timeline and milestones (10 min)
Key dates: [milestone 1 — date], [milestone 2 — date], [launch — date].

Risks and assumptions (10 min)
What could go wrong, and what we are assuming to be true.

Ways of working (5 min)
How often we meet, where the files live, who decides what.

Next steps
Owner and date for every action agreed today.

Board Meeting Agenda

Use this when: a formal board meeting

Call to order and apologies (5 min)
Chair opens the meeting. Record who is present and who sent apologies.

Minutes of the last meeting (5 min)
Approve the minutes. Note any matters arising.

Chief executive's report (15 min)
Performance since the last meeting, against plan.

Finance report (15 min)
Current position, forecast, and any spending that needs approval.

Decisions (20 min)
[Decision 1 — paper attached]. [Decision 2 — paper attached].

Risk and compliance (10 min)
New risks, regulatory changes, anything the board must note.

Any other business (5 min)
Items raised in advance only.

Date of next meeting
Confirm the date and close the meeting.

Client Meeting Agenda

Use this when: a meeting with a client

Welcome and introductions (5 min)
Thank the client for their time. Confirm what we want from the meeting.

Progress since last time (10 min)
What we delivered, what the client delivered, what is outstanding.

Current work (15 min)
Where each open item stands, and what happens next.

Decisions needed from the client (10 min)
[Decision 1]. [Decision 2]. Be specific about what you need.

Feedback (5 min)
Ask how the work is going from the client's side.

Next steps (5 min)
Who does what, by when. Confirm the date of the next meeting.

Daily Standup Agenda

Use this when: the daily 15-minute standup

What did you do yesterday?
One or two sentences each. No detail — save it for after the standup.

What will you do today?
The one or two things that matter today.

Is anything blocking you?
Name the blocker and who can help. Discuss it after the standup, not during it.

Announcements (1 min)
Anything the whole team needs to know today.

Good to know

  • Send the agenda the day before, not the morning of the meeting.
  • Put the decision you need in the subject line or at the top of the agenda.
  • Keep it to five topics — more than that and the meeting runs over.
  • Attach the minutes template afterwards — the standard minutes template and action-item tracker are in the Pro Pack.
