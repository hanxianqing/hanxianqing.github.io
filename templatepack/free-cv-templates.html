Questions

What is the difference between a CV and a resume?

A resume is a short, tailored summary - usually one page - for a specific job. A CV is a fuller record of your career, often two pages or more, and in academia it includes publications, teaching and grants.

Should I use Europass?

For applications in Europe, yes - employers and institutions recognise the format instantly. Outside Europe it looks bureaucratic; use a cleaner two-page layout instead.

How long can a CV be?

Two pages is the norm for most jobs. Academic and research CVs run longer because they list publications. Length is fine when every line is evidence; padding is not.
