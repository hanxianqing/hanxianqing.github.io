Questions

Are the free printables really free?

Yes. The free samples and the two generators are free with no sign-up and no email. The paid bundles are the full sets — 7 to 10 games, or the whole 39-page planner.

What do I get after I buy?

An instant download from Payhip: PDF files (and a spreadsheet with the planner) that you print at home. No physical item is shipped.

What size paper do I need?

US Letter (8.5 x 11 in). The PDFs also print fine on A4 at 100% scale.

Can I use these at school or work?

Yes, for personal and classroom use. Please don't resell or share the files.

Can I share the files with friends?

The files are for personal and classroom use — print as many copies as you need for yourself, but please don't resell or share the files.

Which bundle should I buy first?

For Christmas Day with family, the Christmas Printables Mega Pack ($7, 18 pages). For a party with friends, the Christmas Party Games Bundle ($6, 8 games). Not sure? Print the free sample first — every bundle has one.

What if a file won't print?

Message us through Payhip (use the contact link on the product page) and tell us which file it is. We will send you a replacement.

Do I need a Payhip account to buy?

No. Payhip checkout works without an account — your download links appear on the confirmation page and arrive by email too. Keep the email so you can download again later.

Can I print at a print shop instead?

Yes. The PDFs print at any print shop — US Letter size, print at 100% scale ("Actual size").
