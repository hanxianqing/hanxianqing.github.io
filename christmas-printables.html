How to play

  1. Buy and download. Right after checkout, Payhip shows your download links and emails them to you too — the 6 PDFs and the spreadsheet arrive instantly.
  2. Print at 100% (Actual size) on US Letter paper — never “Fit to page”, or the cards and tags come out the wrong size. Use card stock for the gift tags and bingo cards so they survive the party.
  3. Cut out the gift tags and the 30 bingo calling cards. Fold the calling cards and drop them in a bowl; keep one answer key for the host.
  4. Play in this order: bingo as a warm-up, trivia after dinner, scavenger hunt for the kids while the adults talk — and hand the kids the colouring pages and word search whenever they need a quiet minute.
  5. Score and celebrate. Trivia is 1 point per correct answer; bingo is 5 in a row (or a full-card blackout for a longer game). Hand out a small prize — candy canes or an ornament — after each game.

Party tips

  • Pick 2–3 games, not all of them — a short, lively session beats a long, tired one.
  • Have small prizes ready: candy canes, ornaments, or a silly trophy for the bingo winner.
  • Print the night before, not the morning of — printers always jam on Christmas Eve.
  • One answer key stays with the host; players get game sheets only.
