How to use the pack

  1. Download right after checkout — the PDFs and spreadsheet arrive instantly from Payhip.
  2. Print at 100% (Actual size) on US Letter paper. Use card stock for the gift tags and bingo cards so they survive the party.
  3. Cut out the gift tags and bingo calling cards; keep one answer key for the host.
  4. Play in this order: bingo as a warm-up, trivia after dinner, scavenger hunt for the kids while the adults talk.

Party tips

  • Pick 2–3 games, not all of them — a short, lively session beats a long, tired one.
  • Have small prizes ready: candy canes, ornaments, or a silly trophy for the bingo winner.
  • Print the night before, not the morning of — printers always jam on Christmas Eve.
  • One answer key stays with the host; players get game sheets only.
