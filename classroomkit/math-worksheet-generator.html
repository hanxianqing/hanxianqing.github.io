Questions

Which grades is this for?

Grades 1–6. Use the 1–10 range for early grades, 1–20 for grade 2–3, and 1–100 or the multiplication tables for grades 3–6.

Can I mix operations?

Yes. Choose “Mixed” and the sheet mixes addition, subtraction, multiplication and division.

How many questions can I make?

10, 20 or 30 questions per sheet. Generate a fresh sheet as often as you like — the numbers change every time.

Does it include division with remainders?

No — division questions are exact (for example 42 ÷ 7), so every answer is a whole number. That keeps the sheet right for grades 3–4.

Is there an answer key?

Yes. Tick “Include answer key” and the key prints below the questions, or use the “Show answer key” button after generating.
