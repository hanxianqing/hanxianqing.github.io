Questions

How many cards can I make?

Up to 30 unique cards in one click. Every card is different — the generator checks each new card against the ones already made.

Are the cards unique?

Yes. No two cards in a set are the same. If you ask for more cards than your word list allows, the generator stops at the number it can make uniquely.

Can I use numbers instead of words?

Yes. Type numbers one per line — for example multiplication answers or numbers 1–75 — and the cards use those instead.

Can I print in black and white?

Yes. The cards are black on white with a plain grid, so they print cleanly on any printer.

Do I need an account?

No. The generator runs entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded and nothing is saved.
