Questions

How many words fit?

Start with 5–15 word-and-clue pairs. Most sets of 8–12 words fit on the 15×15 grid; very long words (over 15 letters) cannot fit.

What if a word won't fit?

The tool tells you which words it could not place. Remove or shorten one of them — or pick a word that shares a letter with the words already placed — and generate again.

Can I print the answer key separately?

Yes. Press “Show answers” to fill the grid, then print — the filled grid is your key. Print again with answers hidden for the pupils' copy.

What makes a good clue?

A short definition works best: “A yellow fruit” for BANANA, or “7 × 8” for FIFTYSIX. Keep clues to one line.

Is it really free?

Yes. No sign-up, no email, no watermark — make as many crosswords as you like.
