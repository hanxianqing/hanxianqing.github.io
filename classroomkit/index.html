Questions teachers ask

Are the generators really free?

Yes. Every generator on this site is free to use as often as you like, with no sign-up, no email and no watermark on what you print.

Yes. Every generator produces an answer key with the puzzle or worksheet — the word search, bingo caller sheet, math answers and crossword solution are all included.

Can I print the puzzles?

Yes. Each tool has a Print button. The printout is black-and-white friendly and hides the website around the puzzle, so only the puzzle and its key print.

No. Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded anywhere — close the page and it is gone.
