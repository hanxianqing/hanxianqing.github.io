Questions

How many words can I use?

Between 3 and 20 words, each 2 to 15 letters long. Longer lists need a bigger grid — try 14×14 for 20 words.

Can I print the puzzle?

Yes. Press “Print puzzle” and only the puzzle prints — the website around it is hidden. Print the answer key by pressing “Show answers” first, then printing.

Is there an answer key?

Yes. Press “Show answers” to highlight every hidden word in the grid, then print if you want a key on paper.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. The generator runs in your browser on any device, and the puzzle prints from a phone or tablet the same way.

Is it really free?

Yes. No sign-up, no email, no watermark — make as many puzzles as you like.
