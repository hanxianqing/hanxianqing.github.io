Questions

Does a sponsored card affect rankings or recommendations?

No. Sponsored cards are always labelled Sponsored and sit alongside real content. Rankings and recommendations are ordered by real data and are never for sale.

What happens if my card is taken down before it expires?

We refund you for the remaining days, pro-rata, via PayPal. For example, if a 30-day $12 card is taken down after 10 days, we refund $8 for the 20 days left.

Where does my card appear?

Starter and Standard cards appear in the sponsored slot on this site. Featured cards go first — top placement — for 30 days.

How fast does my sponsorship go live?

Every submission is reviewed by a person, not a machine. If it passes, it goes live within 24 hours after we confirm your PayPal payment.

What content can I not sponsor?

No adult, gambling, crypto-giveaway, scam or illegal content. No link shorteners — link to your real site. We reject anything that breaks these rules and refund in full.

Do you guarantee clicks or traffic?

No. Sponsorship buys placement, not a guaranteed number of clicks. The live board above shows our real traffic, updated daily from Cloudflare — this is a new site and the numbers are still building up.

How do I pay, and what receipt do I get?

You pay with PayPal Smart Buttons at checkout; the amount is locked to the tier you picked and cannot be changed. PayPal sends your receipt, and the transaction id is attached to your request automatically.

Can I change my card after it goes live?

Yes — submit a new request and write that it is an update to a live card. Changes are reviewed the same way before they go live.

Can I renew when my sponsorship ends?

Yes — submit a new request when it expires and it goes live again after review. Cards drop off automatically on their expiry date.

What if my content is rejected?

We refund your payment in full via PayPal. Rejections are rare and only happen when content breaks the rules above.

Do you publish traffic numbers?

Yes — the board at the top of this page, updated daily from Cloudflare Web Analytics. We do not invent or round up traffic figures.

Bigger campaign or a studio? Tell us.

For multi-site buys, longer runs or a custom placement, submit a sponsorship request through the form and write “Custom campaign” in the description. We reply by email within 2 working days.
