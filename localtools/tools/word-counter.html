How it works

Three steps, no account, no waiting room.

  1. Paste your text — an essay, a post, a script, anything.

  3. Check reading time — estimated at 200 words per minute.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a word?

Any run of characters separated by spaces or line breaks. Numbers count as words, just like in most word processors.

Is my text uploaded?

No. Counting happens in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.

How is reading time estimated?

At 200 words per minute, the average adult reading speed. Speaking time is slower — roughly 150 words per minute.

Is there a text size limit?

No. Paste a whole book if you like; counting is instant.

Does it count hyphenated words as one or two?

As one word — a hyphenated word is a single run of characters with no space in it, the same rule most word processors use.

Can I count words in another language?

Yes. Counting is based on whitespace, so it works for any language that separates words with spaces.
